BACKGROUND & PHILOSOPHY

About Me

Each success I have had in school I always find myself with one thing in mind: How can I better understand and solve problems? How can I apply these concepts to the real world? From a young age, I always found myself asking questions about how things work on paper and in the real world. My love for solving problems became my main focus in school, which helped guide me towards what I would want to study as a freshman in college.

From a young age, I loved solving puzzles and taking on mental challenges. As I got older, these mental challenges turned into school competitions. During my time in high school, I took part in several regional and national math and science competitions, as well as Extemporaneous Speaking. Not only did I learn about mathematics and sciences, but I learned how to tackle tough problems. Competitions such as the Philippine Mathematics Olympiad and many school quiz bees have helped me develop the habit of taking difficult problems and finding ways to divide them into smaller, easy steps to solve. Along with solving difficult problems through Math and Science competitions, I have also found myself learning how to speak in public and present ideas that I found through research—all of which will help me become an engineer.

Not only did I like math and science competitions, but I also wanted to put my knowledge into practice. During my time in high school, I helped complete many research initiatives on STEM-based topics. One of my proudest accomplishments in high school was helping complete Project SAGIP-ANI, an eco-friendly research-based project to preserve grains using cheap alternative materials: Coconut Charcoal and Rice Husk Ash Desiccant. This allowed me to see what my school can learn about and put to use in my community here in Nueva Ecija. Whether it's formulas or chemistry, we can all find ways to use it in the real world. Ever since that project, I knew I wanted to spend my life trying to better others' lives by creating something that solves problems.

Why AI Engineering?

Why AI Engineering, you may ask? Well, I have taken math competitions, done science research, and learned about engineering. Combining these interests has led me to believe that I want to learn and advance new technology that will shape our world in the near future.

When I decided what major to take in college, I knew that AI Engineering was my answer. Our world is changing by using Artificial Intelligence to help us solve difficult problems that can benefit us in every aspect of life—medicine, weather forecasting, coding, and more. But as we know, we still need to develop AI that we can depend on. That's why I decided to study AI Engineering. I want to learn how we can better develop AI that can benefit us in the future.

As I grow as a freshman student in the field of AI Engineering, I hope to combine my competition background with learning how to code and advance my skillset to the point where I can create software that betters our world—helping others by using Artificial Intelligence to better solve problems that benefit our day-to-day lives.
